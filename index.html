How I Investigate & Mitigate Financial Crime

An interactive breakdown of my methodical end-to-end investigation framework—from alert triage and adverse media screening to UBO unwrapping and SAR/STR narrative drafting.

Phase 1: Automated Ingestion & Triage

Alert Generation, Sanctions & Watchlist Filtering

Incoming alerts triggered by transaction monitoring thresholds, PEP lists, OFAC/EU sanctions, or adverse media hits are systematically cataloged. I immediately distinguish benign operational friction from authentic financial crime red flags to minimize false positives.

✔ Screening Databases: Cross-referencing entities against OFAC SDN, UN lists, and domestic watchlists via CSI and LexisNexis.
✔ PEP Identification: Analyzing Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs), family members, and close associates (RCAs) to evaluate exposure to corruption.
✔ False-Positive Suppression: Filtering out phonetic misspellings, common namesakes, and outdated hits using secondary identifiers (DOB, passport, address).

Phase 2: Customer & Enhanced Due Diligence

Entity Unwrapping & UBO Verification

Executing rigorous Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) to pierce complex corporate veils. When dealing with opaque legal vehicles, shell corporations, or foreign holding companies, I trace ultimate natural owners possessing 10%–25%+ equity.

✔ Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO): Unpacking tiered shareholding arrangements to identify true controlling minds behind offshore entities.
✔ Adverse Media Intelligence: Screening news portals, court registries, and regulatory enforcement databases for allegations of fraud, bribery, or tax evasion.
✔ Source of Wealth (SoW) & Funds (SoF): Establishing legitimacy of customer net worth and verifying economic rationale for transactional turnover.

Phase 3: Deep Behavioral Forensic

Transaction Analysis & AML Typologies

Leveraging high-volume data analysis to contrast current customer transactional flow against historical declared business profiles, peer group norms, and FATF recognized typologies.

⚠️ Structuring / Smurfing

Multiple deposits just beneath mandatory reporting thresholds (e.g. $9,800) within 72 hours across different branches.

⚠️ Layering via Pass-Through

Rapid wire inflows promptly wired out to unrelated foreign accounts with no legitimate trade rationale or balance retention.

⚠️ High-Risk Jurisdictions

Funds originating or routing through FATF Grey/Black listed jurisdictions or offshore secrecy havens without documented supply chains.

⚠️ Shell Company Markers

Absence of typical operational expenses (payroll, utilities, physical rent) while executing multi-million dollar wires.

VELOCITY & DEVIATION HEATMAP DEVIATION: +340%
Expected Mo. Volume: $10,000 - $25,000
Actual 30-Day Activity: $115,200 (Outlier)

Analysis indicates classic layering symptoms. Customer provided invoice lacks counterparty tax ID and delivery bills of lading.

Phase 4: Regulatory Closure & Escalation

Concise Narrative Writing & SAR / STR Preparation

Transforming hours of transactional and CDD forensics into an objective, factual, and audit-ready investigation narrative structured around the 5 W’s (Who, What, When, Where, Why, and How) for internal MLRO, QA, and regulatory authorities.

✔ The 5 W’s Methodology: Articulating the clear nexus between alert triggers, anomalous movement, and underlying economic illegitimacy.
✔ SAR/STR Justification: Providing unambiguous, defensible rationale when recommending case escalation or filing of Suspicious Activity Reports.
✔ Audit & QA Benchmark: Meeting 100% compliance quality standards with zero tolerance for unverified assumptions or incomplete trails.
